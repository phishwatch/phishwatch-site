1. What this agreement covers

PhishWatch grants the Partner a free, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to deploy and evaluate the PhishWatch browser security extension for a period of 30 days (the "Pilot"). The Pilot covers deployment to one client company chosen by the Partner.

This agreement begins on the date both parties sign it and ends automatically after 30 days unless extended in writing by both parties.

2. What PhishWatch commits to

  • Provide access to the PhishWatch extension and MSP dashboard at no charge
  • Review and respond to any false positive report within one business day
  • Provide email support throughout the Pilot period
  • Deliver a written summary report within 5 business days of the Pilot ending
  • Process all data in accordance with the GDPR Data Processing Agreement [for counsel: where the agreement is published or attached]

3. What the Partner commits to

  • Deploy PhishWatch to at least one client company during the Pilot period
  • Keep telemetry enabled so the dashboard has data to report on
  • Provide honest feedback on what works and what does not
  • Not use PhishWatch for any purpose other than evaluation during the Pilot
  • Inform their client that PhishWatch will be deployed and what data it processes, as described in Section 4

4. Data processing

When a local check in the browser finds a reason to look closer, the extension sends the address of the link to PhishWatch's server for scoring. For a link, this is the full address, without the part after # and without any password written into it. For the page the user is on, it is the address without the values in its query string. The extension also sends the names of the warning signs it found with short details about them, a random ID for each page load and the Partner's key. PhishWatch's server may open the address to follow its redirects, with token-like query parameters removed. No page content, passwords, clipboard text, user names or email addresses are transmitted or stored.

PhishWatch stores, for the Partner's devices only: the domain of each checked address (never the full address), the risk result, signal names and a timestamp, deleted after 30 days; and, where telemetry is on, events with the domain, risk result, signal names, the user's choice on a warning and the page-load ID, plus daily counts of checks and failed checks, deleted after 90 days. Data is stored in the Frankfurt, Germany region of PhishWatch's hosting provider, Render. Requests pass through Cloudflare's network, and email alerts are sent through Gmail.

The GDPR Art. 28 Data Processing Agreement [for counsel: where published or attached] is incorporated by reference.

5. No charge, no warranty

The Pilot is provided entirely free of charge. PhishWatch is provided "as is" during the Pilot. No uptime guarantee, no SLA, and no warranty of any kind is given. PhishWatch's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed [for counsel: amount].

Note for counsel (remove before use): the previous text capped liability at EUR 0. Facts: free pilot; the provider is a sole trader in Berlin; the partners are DACH MSPs; the product fails open by design: when the server cannot be reached or times out, the user is not blocked and no warning is shown. Options: a fixed amount; a cap tied to insurance cover; statutory liability only with no stated cap. Whether a zero cap is effective under the governing law (Section 9) is the question.

Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages arising from the use or inability to use PhishWatch during the Pilot.

6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential information private. This includes: the Partner's client details and deployment configuration, and PhishWatch's technical architecture and commercial terms. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for two years.

7. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement at any time with 24 hours written notice to the other party. On termination, the Partner removes PhishWatch from all deployed devices via the same Chrome policy used to install it. PhishWatch will delete all Partner data within [for counsel: deadline] of a written deletion request to security@phishwatch.io.

Note for counsel (remove before use): this agreement said 72 hours; the DPA template (Article 8) said 30 days. Facts: deletion of a tenant is one admin action that removes all of its database records in one transaction, run by hand by the founder. It does not reach server logs, emails already sent or provider backups. Options: 72 hours for the database with a separate period for logs, mail and backups; 30 days for everything; or both numbers per data type. The same number must appear here and in the DPA.

8. What happens after the Pilot

This Agreement does not create any obligation for either party to enter a commercial relationship after the Pilot. If the Partner wishes to continue using PhishWatch, a separate commercial agreement will be required.

9. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by [for counsel: governing law]. Any disputes shall be resolved by [for counsel: courts], except that either party may seek urgent injunctive relief in any jurisdiction.
